01 / Our mission

The human brain isn’t one-size-fits-all.
Our data shouldn’t be either.

A better understanding of the brain starts with a broader range of people.

Our ambition is to build the world’s largest neural databank. Not just more data, but data that reflects more of humanity: different people, communities, and lived experiences.

We’re focusing on underrepresented demographics to build a more representative foundation for neuroscience. By bringing data collection to more people, we aim to make the human picture more complete.

More people. Richer neural data.

02 / Our approach
